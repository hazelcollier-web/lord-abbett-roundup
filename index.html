More key words

    
    
For when the short list isn't turning anything up.


    

      
A"Asset management"

      
    
        
  • Deal words: acquire, buy, sale, deal, merger, stake, takeover
    • 
        
  • Money flows: AUM, assets under management, inflows, outflows, redemptions, withdrawals
    • 
        
  • Big names: BlackRock, Vanguard, Pimco, Franklin, Blackstone, KKR, Apollo, Blue Owl
    • 
        
  • Industry shifts: consolidation, scale, fee cuts, private markets, wealth management
    • 
      

    

    

      
B"Mutual funds"

      
    
        
  • active vs. passive, stock-picking, beat benchmark, Morningstar
    • 
        
  • ETF, launches, conversion, share class
    • 
        
  • flows, outflows, fees, interval fund, retail investors, 401(k), target-date
    • 
        
  • SEC, rule, disclosure
    • 
      

    

    

      
C"Capital markets"

      
    
        
  • Rates: Treasuries, yields, 10-year, 30-year, bond selloff, Fed, rate hike, Warsh, buyback
    • 
        
  • Credit: spreads, high yield, junk, leveraged loans, private credit, default, bond sale, issuance
    • 
        
  • Munis: municipal, muni
    • 
        
  • Deals: IPO, listing, record deal
    • 
      

    

    

      
★Probably a keeper, in any search

      
    
        
  • record, biggest, first since, highest/lowest since
    • 
        
  • a dollar figure: $4bn, $30 billion
    • 
        
  • curbs, halts, limits, warns
